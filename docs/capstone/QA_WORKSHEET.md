# Personal Q&A worksheet

These answers must be written and spoken by the presenter. Keep each to one or
two complete sentences, include the question in the answer, and use only facts
that are personally true.

## 1

**What made you decide to pursue AI Engineering — and what almost held you
back?**

Answer frame:

> What made me decide to pursue AI Engineering was ______, and what almost held
> me back was ______. I decided to move forward because ______.

## 2

**Was there a moment, project, or person during the program when things really
clicked for you?**

Answer frame:

> The moment during the program when things really clicked for me was ______,
> because ______.

## 3

**What would you tell someone who's on the fence about joining 4Geeks?**

Answer frame:

> What I would tell someone who's on the fence about joining 4Geeks is ______,
> because my experience showed me ______.

Pause silently for two full seconds before and after each final answer. Look at
the lens for the entire Q&A section.
